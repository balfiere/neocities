{% resourcesContainer "tools" %}{% renderTemplate "md" %}

[uBlock Origin](https://ublockorigin.com/){target='_blank'}
: a lot of following sites and their file hosters have intrusive ads to help pay for their server costs; be sure to have a pop up blocker installed to make your browsing experience easier.

[FastForward](https://fastforward.team/){target='_blank'}
: extension that bypasses link shorteners and other sites that make you wait 5+ seconds to get to your download link.

[Mullvad VPN](https://mullvad.net/en){target='_blank'}
: if you want to torrent, be sure to have a vpn installed so you don't get copyright infringement notices from your isp (unless your country doesn't care about that). this is the one i recommend since it has a cheap flat rate of €5/month and doesn't log user data.
