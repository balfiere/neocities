{% resourcesContainer "hacking the psp" %}{% renderTemplate "md" %}

[pspunk](https://www.pspunk.com/){target='_blank'}
: website with a bunch of easy to follow guides on modding your psp. you can find their guide to installing custom firmware [here](https://www.pspunk.com/psp-cfw/){target='_blank'} and links to useful homebrew apps [here](https://www.pspunk.com/psp-homebrew/){target='_blank'}.

{% endrenderTemplate %}{% endresourcesContainer %}


{% resourcesContainer "emulating the psp" %}{% renderTemplate "md" %}

[PPSSPP](https://www.ppsspp.org/){target='_blank'}
: psp emulator. officially supports android, windows, linux, and mac, but also has builds for ios, switch, and other platforms.

{% endrenderTemplate %}{% endresourcesContainer %}


{% resourcesContainer "psp games" %}{% renderTemplate "md" %}

[NoPayStation](https://nopaystation.com/faq){target='_blank'}
: download psp games straight from the playstation server.follow the 'configuration and usage' section, following the areas that say 'PSP', to set up NPS Browser on your computer. you can also download .pkg files straight from their site under the [browse](https://nopaystation.com/browse){target='_blank'} section. be sure to use [this plugin](https://github.com/qwikrazor87/npdrm_free){target='_blank'} to get the files working on a modded psp.

[Myrient](https://myrient.erista.me/files/Redump/Sony%20-%20PlayStation%20Portable/){target='_blank'}
: complete collection of psp games for every region in .iso format
